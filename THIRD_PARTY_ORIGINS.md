# Third-party origins

The audit used third-party algorithm archives as external evidence objects.
They are **not redistributed** in this repository; they retain their original
licences and source attribution. Re-obtain them from their official
distributions and verify against the SHA-256 hashes recorded in the
companion reproducibility package:

| Archive | Origin | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|
| LSHADE1.0.1_CEC2014_Octave-Matlab.zip | L-SHADE 1.0.1, Tanabe & Fukunaga (CEC 2014), Octave/Matlab competition archive; author-corrected copy used for the dependency-complete positive case | a9d094018b18da674849a97415116146dae6e423c19be8ef0ada9766890f0b9b |
| LSHADE_SPACMA_1.0.zip | LSHADE-SPACMA (CEC 2017 competition distribution) | 3bbde55b92b2e4be9bcbf957b94f5e0e9d9aa09ebad70291909c718a661d4c02 |
| NL-SHADE-RSP.zip | NL-SHADE-RSP, Stanovov, Akhmedova & Semenkin; CEC 2021 competition code, http://www.ntu.edu.sg/home/epnsugan/index_files/CEC2017/CEC2017.htm | aaa61f80a1358142b0c790afe539d52a1df54073c074c3ede18a01c44f4afa58 |

The jSO official source (Brest & Maucec, CEC 2017) was likewise used as an
official-source runnability evidence object and is documented with its hash
in the reproducibility package accompanying the manuscript.
